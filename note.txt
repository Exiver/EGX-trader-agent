Personal trading rule: I'm cautious about EGX banking stocks (COMI, HDBK,
QNBE, CANA) during periods of EGP currency volatility, since their margins
are sensitive to interest rate moves. I prefer to wait for a clear signal
rather than trade them on ambiguous days.

I'm generally bullish on Egyptian real estate developers (TMGH, EMFD, PHDC,
ORHD) given ongoing urban development projects, but I watch for
overextension after big single-day rallies.
